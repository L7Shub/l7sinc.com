Last updated: October 24, 2026

This notice supplements the L7S, Inc. Privacy Policy for residents of California under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, "CCPA").

Directly from you when you fill out a form, book a call or purchase, and automatically from your browser when you use our website.

L7S does not sell or rent your personal information. To opt out of marketing communications, use the unsubscribe link in any email or email admin@l7sinc.com.

Your California rights

  • The right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose.
  • The right to delete personal information we collected from you.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • The right not to receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

How to submit a request

Email admin@l7sinc.com with the subject "California privacy request". We verify your identity before acting on a request and respond within 45 days; if we need more time we will tell you. You may use an authorized agent, who must show your written permission.
